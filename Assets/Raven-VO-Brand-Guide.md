# Raven VO — Brand Guide

Version 1.0 · October 27, 2026

## Family and purpose

Raven VO is the local voice-over creation app in the RavenMix / RavenAudio family. The owner-confirmed family reference is RavenMix Brand Guide v1.0 (September 22, 2026). Raven VO is the exact product spelling; VO is uppercase and separated by a space. Website: https://www.ravenvo.app.

## Mark

Use AppIcon.png or AppIcon.icns: the same friendly full-body black raven facing right, amber studio headphones, white wing separation and white support tile, differentiated by three red speech waves. The voice-over mark omits RavenMix's viewfinder corners and Rec lettering. Speech waves represent the product category; they are not an authoritative recording or generation status indicator.

Keep the supplied light tile in dark interfaces. Preserve clear space, the complete raven, and the speech waves. Do not mirror or stretch, add an aggressive head, glowing eye, gradient feathers, runes, or extra accessories. Use a native system sans-serif for supporting text and a bold product name. Do not invent a named font for the raster artwork.

Generated raster colors may vary slightly; these are the exact UI tokens. Keep labels/icons alongside state colors. The app uses native system text and surfaces so dark mode remains readable; the mark itself retains its white tile.
